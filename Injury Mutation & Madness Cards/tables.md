# Injury

Number rolled	Name	Description (green text)	Details (black text)
1–2	Ripped Apart	You are torn to pieces or crushed beyond recovery. It is a sad day when a Hero falls.	Your Hero is Dead.
3	Internal Bleeding	Your condition is serious! Wiping blood from your mouth, you assure everyone that you are fine.	Any further Injury Chart rolls are only made on a single D6. This Injury is –1 to the roll to Heal during Surgery at the Doc’s Office in Town.
4	Mangled Hand	One of your hands is twisted and broken. It’s hard to even hold anything in it.	You have one less slot for equipping Items each turn. Two-handed Weapons may still be used, but they cannot get Critical Hits.
5	Gouged Eye	Ouch! Your vision is blurred and cloudy as one of your eyes has been scratched!	You cannot get Critical Hits with your Ranged Attacks.
6	Slashed Leg	A deep and painful cut runs across your leg, making it hard to stand let alone to walk.	You are –1 Move (minimum of 1).
7	Concussion	Your ears are ringing a little and you have a headache that won’t quit!	Until the start of the next Adventure, you are –1 Initiative and roll one less dice on all Skill Tests.
8	Crushed Arm	Your arm has been bent and crushed a bit. It may even be broken.	You are –1 Combat.
9	Broken Collar Bone	Not particularly debilitating, but it sure hurts! You find yourself cringing a lot from the pain.	You are –1 Max Grit.
10	Chest Wound	Deep gashes run across your chest, making it hard to turn or twist your body; it’s kind of painful to even breathe.	You are –1 Initiative.
11	Wind Knocked Out	You pick yourself up and shake yourself off! It’s a little hard to catch your breath, but you’ll be ok.	There is no long lasting effect.
12	New Scars	You’re a bit of a mess, but it’s all superficial. In a lot of ways, you look tougher!	You are +1 Max Grit.

# Madness

Number rolled	Name	Description (green text)	Details (black text)
1–2	Lost to the Darkness	You have descended into the dark depths of purest Madness! With a final quiver, you crumple to the floor and slip into a catatonic state that you are unlikely to ever recover from.	Your Hero is, for all intents and purposes, considered to be **Dead**.
3	Delusions	Your fractured state of mind is in shambles, and you are ready to slip over the edge at any moment.	Any further Madness Chart rolls are only made on a single D6. This Madness is –1 to the roll to Heal during an Exorcism of Madness at the Church in Town.
4	The Itching	Your skin burns and itches as you scratch at it feverishly. Why isn’t anyone else scratching?!?	At the start of each turn, you take 1 Hit, as you claw at your skin.
5	It’s Coming for You!	How does it know your name?!? You can hear it calling you in the dark! What does it want?	Whenever the Hold Back the Darkness roll is failed (including doubles that would fail), you take D3 Sanity Damage, ignoring Willpower saves.
6	Paranoia	They’re out to get you…all of them! Just look at that little one, with the beady eyes! They want to kill you…and take your stuff for themselves! Maybe they’re all demons…they’re all possessed!!!	Any time you want to Scavenge or Explore a Doorway, you must first roll a D6. On the roll of 1, 2 or 3, you cannot, as you are too distracted keeping an eye on the other Heroes’ motives.
7	Open to the Void	You feel the ebb and flow of the Void energy all around you, seeping into your mind and clawing at your deepest fears!	You no longer get a Willpower save to prevent Corruption Hits.
8	The Chills	It’s…soooo…cooold in here! It feels like you are freezing…on the inside!	When Scavenging, ignore the first 6 you roll on the Scavenge test.
9	Hallucinations	You can no longer trust what you see as shadows and tendrils of darkness writhe in every corner of your eye.	Any time you would draw a Loot card, roll a D6. On the roll of 1, you instead take 1 Sanity Damage, ignoring Willpower, as you recoil from the hideous visions!
10	Hearing Voices	They’re telling you to kill…again! Any time you end your Move adjacent to another Hero, roll a D6.	On the roll of 1 or 2, that Hero takes 3 Hits as you lash out at them!
11	Get a Grip!	You shake it off. You’re fine…everything’s fine, right?	There is no long lasting effect.
12	I’ve Seen Things…!	You’ve come back from the brink of madness…and you are stronger for it!	You are +1 Max Grit.
