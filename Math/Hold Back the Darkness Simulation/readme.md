# Darkness Thread

In how many turns the darkness, on average, will leave the mines?

This is a python script to excute a montecarlo simulation of sequential Hold back the darkness tests.

On average, in 39 turns 50% of time the darkness will escape. Playing less than 30 turns will be extremly rare (oh bad luck!) as more than 50 aswell (luck party).

Of course, grit, artifacts, mission rules, darkness cards and other things can an will change this average. And the faster the party goes down the mines, the faster it will raise the threshold of the darkness (from 7+ to 8+, 9+, etc).
