# Probability Rolls

What is the probability to pass a 5+ Roll if you have 3 for the skill? That is, roll 3 D6 dices and roll at least one 5 or 6? What is better? Roll 6+ in 4 dices or a 5+ in two?

The spreadsheet answer that. (answers: 70% and roll two 5+)

The second part is when you roll for a D8 (for U. S. Marshall's attacks)
